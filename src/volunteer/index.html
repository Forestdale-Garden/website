Volunteer

Love the idea of contributing to the pocket forest? Here are the best ways to get involved. You can also join our Facebook group to stay in the loop on upcoming events and volunteer opportunities.

  • Plant something. Join us for a planting day and help put new trees, shrubs, or groundcover in the ground. No experience needed — we'll show you what to do.
  • Donate a plant. Check out our Plant Wishlist and donate a plant we're hoping to add to the forest. It's one of the most direct ways to make a lasting impact.
  • Help with cleanup and maintenance. Join a group event to water, weed, pull invasives, and keep the space looking its best — or stop by on your own schedule and do a little light care whenever you're passing by. Family-friendly and low-commitment.
